How to fly it

  1. Hold R to bring the throttle to full and let the aircraft roll down the runway.
  2. At about 55 knots, ease back (S or ↓) to lift the nose — it will climb away on its own.
  3. Bank with A / D to turn. The orange marker and the minimap point to the next ring.
  4. Pull too hard or fly too slow and the wing stalls — push the nose down to recover.
  5. The turn coordinator (bottom left) shows your rate of turn: wings on the L or R mark is a standard-rate turn, a full circle in two minutes. Keep the ball centred with the rudder (Q / E) — if it drifts, press the rudder on the ball's side.
  6. To land, line up with the runway, slow to about 60 knots and touch down gently: descent under 880 ft/min, wings level.
