Review

What you must be able to do

3.6 Cauchy–Euler: $ax^2y''+bxy'+cy=g(x)$

  • Try $y=x^m$: auxiliary equation $am(m-1)+bm+c=0$. Watch the $m(m-1)$, it is not $m^2$.
  • Distinct real roots: $c_1x^{m_1}+c_2x^{m_2}$. Repeated: $c_1x^m+c_2x^m\ln x$. Complex $\alpha\pm i\beta$: $x^\alpha\big(c_1\cos(\beta\ln x)+c_2\sin(\beta\ln x)\big)$.
  • Nonhomogeneous: divide by $ax^2$ first to get standard form, then variation of parameters with $f(x)=\dfrac{g(x)}{ax^2}$.

3.8 Spring–mass and LRC: $my''+\beta y'+ky=f(t)$

  • Hooke’s law gives $k$ from a stretch: $k=\dfrac{mg}{s}$ or $\dfrac{W}{s}$. In imperial units, mass is $m=\dfrac{W}{32}$ slugs.
  • Down is positive. “Released from 2 ft above equilibrium” means $y(0)=-2$.
  • $\beta^2-4mk$: positive overdamped, zero critically damped, negative underdamped.
  • Amplitude form: $c_1\cos\omega t+c_2\sin\omega t=A\sin(\omega t+\phi)$, $A=\sqrt{c_1^2+c_2^2}$, $\tan\phi=\dfrac{c_1}{c_2}$, pick the quadrant from the signs.
  • LRC: $Lq''+Rq'+\dfrac{q}{C}=E(t)$, current $i=q'$.

3.9 Boundary-value problems

  • Beam: $EI\,y^{(4)}=w(x)$. Embedded end: $y=y'=0$. Simply supported: $y=y''=0$. Free: $y''=y'''=0$.
  • Eigenvalue problems $y''+\lambda y=0$: check $\lambda<0$, $\lambda=0$, $\lambda>0$ separately. Report eigenvalues and eigenfunctions.

4.1 to 4.3 Laplace

  • Definition integral for simple or piecewise $f$.
  • Inverse with partial fractions, completing the square and the first translation theorem.
  • IVPs: $\mathcal{L}\{y''\}=s^2Y-sy(0)-y'(0)$.
  • Piecewise $f$ to unit steps; $\mathcal{L}\{g(t)\,\mathcal{U}(t-a)\}=e^{-as}\mathcal{L}\{g(t+a)\}$; $\mathcal{L}^{-1}\{e^{-as}F\}=f(t-a)\,\mathcal{U}(t-a)$.

Traps that cost marks
  • Applying ICs to $y_c$ instead of the full $y=y_c+y_p$.
  • Wrong sign on the initial displacement (above equilibrium is negative).
  • Using pounds as mass. Divide the weight by $32$.
  • Forgetting $\lambda=0$ in an eigenvalue problem.
  • $\mathcal{L}\{t\,\mathcal{U}(t-2)\}$ is $e^{-2s}\mathcal{L}\{t+2\}$, not $e^{-2s}\mathcal{L}\{t\}$.
